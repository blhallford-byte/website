About Us

Christ the Redeemer Christian Academy exists to provide a Christ-centered, biblically grounded education that develops students spiritually, academically, and morally.

Our mission is to raise students with a strong Biblical worldview who are prepared to serve God, their families, and their communities.

ACSI Accredited

Christ the Redeemer Christian Academy is proud to be accredited by the Association of Christian Schools International (ACSI).

ACSI accreditation demonstrates our commitment to educational excellence, Christian values, and rigorous academic standards. Our school meets the highest benchmarks for curriculum, faculty qualifications, and student outcomes.
